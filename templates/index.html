enCAGE Cargo Assessment for Guided Encapsulation

Predict your protein's ferritin encapsulation regime

Upload a PDB structure (or fetch one by ID) to estimate whether a cargo protein is likely to undergo efficient luminal encapsulation (Regime I), steric/structural accommodation (Regime II), or charge-driven off-pathway assembly (Regime III) in apoferritin nanocages, based on net charge, size, and cavity occupancy descriptors.
