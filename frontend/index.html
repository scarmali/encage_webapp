Welcome to enCAGE

Predict the outcome of protein encapsulation in apoferritin nanocages

enCAGE uses a descriptor-guided framework that integrates structural and physicochemical properties to classify the likely assembly regime for your protein of interest — efficient luminal encapsulation, steric/structural accommodation, or charge-driven off-pathway assembly.

Structure-aware Integrates size, shape and Dmax to assess steric compatibility with the ferritin cavity
Charge-informed Considers electrostatic complementarity with the negatively charged apoferritin interior
Descriptor-guided Classifies assembly into interpretable regimes with a clear, hierarchical rationale
Open & web-based Rule-based framework, no installation required — runs directly in your browser

Get started

Upload a .pdb file or enter a PDB ID on the left, then run the analysis. enCAGE is a heuristic design aid, not a validated classifier — regime boundaries are calibrated on a small in-house cargo panel.
